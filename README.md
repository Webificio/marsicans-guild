# Marsicans Guild

**The all-in-one guild companion addon for WoW Forever** (World of Warcraft, interface 1.60.1).

📦 **Download:** [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/marsicans-guild) · ☕ [Support on Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/gabrieleventurini)

Born in the Marsicans guild, made for **any guild**. Install it, type `/mg`, and every guildmate who has it shares their data automatically: no setup, no server, no account.

## What it does

- 🏆 **Guild quests and Karma**: the steps that make someone part of the guild (dungeons with guildmates, clean runs, raid bosses, events) turned into a game with ranks, rewards and a leaderboard
- 🗺️ **Questing together**: which guildmates share your quests, group quest progress, who needs the item you just looted, 560 dungeon quests with full chains for all 37 dungeons
- ⚔️ **Dungeons**: every boss and its loot with drop chances, pre-need reminders, a ready check before the run (`/mg check`), the guild's fastest runs and end-of-run titles with an MVP
- 🧭 **Leveling guide**: a 1-60 route for your race and faction, with goals that tick themselves off and an arrow that follows the real walking path
- 📅 **Events**: 5 and 10 player dungeon nights with roles, reminders, live boss progress and a guild chronicle
- 👥 **Guild roster**: when everyone plays, where they are, roles, alts, who's available right now, and "looking for group" inside the guild (`/mg lfg`)
- ⚒️ **Professions**: who can craft what, and crafting requests to the whole guild
- 📣 **Recruiter**: find guildless players, whisper them, sort the replies, and post channel ads in turns shared by the guild so nobody spams
- 🛡️ **Guild management** for the guild master and officers: overview, member stats, guild board, polls, addon version check
- 🧰 **Quality of life**: auto repair, sell grey items, auto quests, live guild map, gratz on level up (all optional)

Full feature list and screenshots on the [CurseForge page](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/marsicans-guild).
